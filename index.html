012026  /  MLOps & Governance

Continuous Credit Risk Governance Pipeline

A self-governing MLOps pipeline that runs the full credit-risk model lifecycle end to end: drift detection, retraining, statistical gating, promotion, rollback, with no human in the loop.

  • 01637 live batches over 150K+ credit records, zero manual intervention.
  • 02Tuned drift threshold cut false retrain triggers from about 40% to about 1%, without missing real injected drift.
  • 03Three-gate promotion: a tolerance band, strict dominance, and McNemar or bootstrap significance testing.
  • 04Rollback is held to the same bar, confirmed by a bootstrap CI rather than one bad batch.
  • 0555 automated tests audit every governance decision, including the ones that changed nothing.

AUC-PR is tracked over accuracy given the low positive rate, and every decision, including rejections and suppressions, lands in a Postgres audit trail.

Kubernetes Gated RAG

A defense-in-depth RAG system for Kubernetes Q&A, built as a LangGraph state machine that refuses to answer rather than answer wrong.

  • 01Fail-closed safety and topic gates run before any expensive retrieval or generation work.
  • 02Threshold-gated cross-encoder reranking, not just reordering, on a no-GPU 16GB machine.
  • 03Two-layer cache, exact match plus semantic at cosine 0.95 or above, skips redundant work on repeated or paraphrased questions.
  • 04RAGAS evaluation across 6 metrics, judged by a model family isolated from the pipeline it grades.

A three-provider failover chain keeps the system answering even when a single LLM provider is rate-limited or down, with full node- and provider-level tracing via Logfire.

042025  /  Causal Inference

Causal Impact & Heterogeneity Lab

A causal inference pipeline that validates its own estimators against known ground truth before it trusts them on real ad-exposure data.

  • 01OLS, propensity matching, IPW and AIPW compared against a self-induced, calibrated confounder first.
  • 02A minimum-detectable-effect calculation, not a default, decides which outcome variable is usable per segment.
  • 03Benjamini-Hochberg correction controls the false discovery rate across every tested segment.
  • 04Rosenbaum bounds quantify how much unmeasured confounding would be needed to overturn the result.

Matching is strict one-to-one without replacement, so under this dataset's treatment skew most treated units are discarded by design, and the dashboard reports that match rate rather than hiding it.

052025  /  Recommender Systems

ReelBench: Recommender Benchmark

A production-style two-stage recommender on MovieLens 25M, scoring five approaches through one identical, leakage-tested harness.

  • 01Popularity, item-item CF, ALS/BPR, a two-tower retriever and SASRec, all on Recall, NDCG and MAP at 10 and 20.
  • 02A single global timestamp cutoff, not a per-user split, closes a leakage path a per-user check alone would miss.
  • 03Row-blocked sparse similarity keeps item-item CF under 200MB against a projected 15GB dense matrix.
  • 04FAISS retrieval plus a LightGBM reranker served on CPU only, with a separate cold-start index for zero-interaction items.

The committed comparison table currently scores the three CPU baselines; the harness can already score the two neural models, they just haven't been run against a trained checkpoint in this environment.

062026  /  Forecasting & Anomalies

RetailCast: Forecasting & Anomaly Bench

A demand forecasting and anomaly detection bench across 60 retail series, with a narrative layer that verifies its own numbers before showing them.

  • 01Prophet, SARIMA, LightGBM and XGBoost compared with expanding-window walk-forward cross-validation.
  • 02XGBoost wins on holdout at MASE 0.632, ahead of LightGBM at 0.674; both classical models sit at or above MASE 1.0.
  • 03Control limits at k=2.5 beat Isolation Forest on F1, 0.694 against 0.632, driven by higher recall.
  • 04A regex-based grounding check verifies every generated number against source data before the dashboard shows it.

Cost-of-error figures are illustrative, grounded in published grocery-retail margins rather than this business's actual P&L.
